# P3 Final Verification Evidence and Technical Boundaries

**Date:** 2026-11-13 KST  
**Original publication:** K.-L. J. Wong, H. Hatamkhani, M. Mansuri, and C.-K. K. Yang, "A 27-mW 3.6-Gb/s I/O Transceiver," *IEEE Journal of Solid-State Circuits*, vol. 39, no. 4, pp. 602–612, Apr. 2004, doi: 10.1109/JSSC.2004.825259.  
**Reconstructed environment:** ngspice 42, XSPICE, DB HiTek 180-nm MOS transistor models, Verilator, Python. These are independently designed circuits; the 2004 author's transistor schematics, foundry process, layout parasitics, channel measurements and HSPICE references are **not known to be equivalent**.

## 0. Executive engineering conclusion

**The exhaustive available evidence supports physically simulated reference-PLL functionality over a complete enumerated process/temperature/supply grid, real MOS transmitter FFE vertical-eye improvements for two independent PRBS31 patterns, transistor-implemented signed receiver-reference correction and partial supply-power comparisons. The independent XSPICE physical-data CDR does NOT demonstrate reliable sustained lock.** In the longer 1024-bit holdout, the tested positive 3-mV/counter-code feedback actually increased error counts in all eight matched controls, whereas earlier 512-bit experiments showed improvements under some conditions. Neither the 2004 silicon's BER, full 27.12-mW power or measured FR4/RG58 channel was reproduced.

### Interpret the word "PASS" carefully

1. **GitHub CI success:** the software test script completed and uploaded evidence. This is not necessarily electrical success. Many exploratory scripts deliberately return zero exit code after writing negative results.
2. **Electrical PASS:** actual physically simulated MOS waveform completed and met numerical criteria fixed *before* evaluating the result.
3. **Pattern-dependent or gain-dependent improvement:** data errors decrease for a particular start phase, but not necessarily to zero or with confirmed CDR phase acquisition.
4. **Original 2004 ASIC equivalence:** requires the actual process, circuit topology, layout, measured channel and measurement conditions; not established.

## 1. Reference PLL: complete Cartesian PVT plus numerical cross-check

### Physical reconstructed circuit

- XSPICE behavioral 450-MHz reference clock PFD /4 divider, actual 7-bit MOS switched charge pump, actual 180-nm diode-connected PMOS source-bias device and resistor, physical second-order RC loop filter, MOS 3-stage current-starved VCO, extra ideal design load 3 fF per VCO stage.
- Two fixed PDK-model designs, **one invariant topology and sizing per full PVT matrix**:
  - **A:** diode PMOS W=16 µm, bias R=4 kΩ, RZERO=1 kΩ.
  - **B:** diode PMOS W=8 µm, bias R=8 kΩ, RZERO=1 kΩ.
- Additional conditions are 82.5%-size CP source/sink devices, VCTRL startup 1.02 V, five process corners TT/FF/SS/FS/SF, three temperatures -20, 27, 85°C, three core supplies 1.71, 1.80, 1.89 V = **45 independent conditions per fixed design**.

### Quantified default 3-ps transient evidence

The saved **90** actual MOS transient JSON reports were independently checked for exactly one record of each tuple, no duplicates or missing PVT points. Results in [90-case independent audit](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43253822240):

| Design | Physical simulations evaluated | Electrical PASS | Maximum phase-edge std | Maximum VCO gate ripple | Maximum three-window frequency span | Last-window cycle slips |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A, W16/R4k | 45 | **45/45** | 2.3922 ps | 0.7805 mVpp | 1.9951 MHz | 0 |
| B, W8/R8k | 45 | **45/45** | 4.9690 ps | 0.7880 mVpp | 1.0690 MHz | 0 |

**Screen:** absolute output error below 1500 ppm from 1.8 GHz, deterministic nearest-reference-edge phase difference std below 8 ps, VCO gate ripple below 5 mVpp, zero final interval slips, and frequency range below 2 MHz across three final disjoint 80-ns windows.

The original "first all-PVT success" for a reference PLL is limited to those *specific modeled, deterministic screens*. It is not random RMS jitter, phase noise, loop gain margin or silicon reliability.

### Independent integration algorithm

A separate **Gear 3-ps** ngspice transient was executed for **design B across all 45 process/temperature/supply Cartesian points**. A first audit mistakenly searched the wrong case-sensitive working-directory path; it falsely reported 0/45 complete. The waveform path was corrected, the full 45 cases were rerun and the independent audit finally verified **45/45 full 1200-ns transients and 45/45 electrical PASS**.

[Validated Gear45 independent audit with corrected claim boundaries](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43267380020), [actual Gear physical simulations](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43262471190).

**Additional stricter time step study:** at the marginal SS, 85°C, VDD=1.71 V design point, Gear 3ps with design B completed and passed (reference phase-edge std approximately 4.977 ps; late frequency span 1.0741 MHz). Gear 1ps ended at approximately 8.690 ns and Gear 2ps ended at approximately 509.850 ns, with no full 1200-ns waveform; thus *those two alternative grids do not qualify*. Design A also failed Gear 3ps late drift (2.0364 MHz, limit 2 MHz). [Fixed Gear refinement test](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43265243046).

**Accurate claim:** "The independently reconstructed physical MOS reference PLL B passed all 45 enumerated PVT conditions using both default 3ps and Gear 3ps transient settings. Convergence and equivalence under every smaller time step remain unverified."

### Reference PLL is not serial-data CDR

These PVT tests compare an actual MOS VCO divided by four against an **ideal 450 MHz reference** using XSPICE PFD. They do not prove a 3.6-Gb/s serial data clock recovered from transitions in the incoming data.

## 2. Physical TX predriver and FFE: independent PRBS31 replication

Two independent nonrepeating **512-bit PRBS31 seeds** with the *same* independent 180-nm CMOS two-stage TX predriver, segmented 8:4:2:1 MOS TX, surrogate two-pole -12-dB at Nyquist RC channel, physically regenerative MOS receiver latches, XSPICE PFD and MOS CP/VCO were simulated.

A common RX sampling delay bias of 1.10 V, **one training-selected clock phase and integer framing for all six tested FFE settings**, and 358 untouched post-training bits were used for analog Eye and physical latch-scoring comparisons.

### Vertical Eye and transmitter power, RX clock bias 1.10 V

| FFE α | Seed A reconstructed analog Eye, mV | Seed B reconstructed analog Eye, mV | Seed A partial supply power, mW |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.0 | 44.818 | 44.855 | 6.586 |
| 0.1 | 55.241 | 55.325 | 6.678 |
| 0.2 | 67.074 | 66.980 | 6.760 |
| 0.3 | 78.137 | 78.195 | 6.831 |
| **0.4** | **85.338** | **85.636** | **6.885** |
| 0.5 | 79.765 | 79.869 | 6.934 |

[First independent MOS pattern](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43254490229), [second independent MOS pattern](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43256064235).

Under the specified surrogate channel and clock, α≈0.4 generated the widest **vertical** Eye across the sampled coefficients and both PRBS31 patterns. **This is not a guaranteed globally optimal FFE, nor an independent proof of horizontal Eye Opening or the paper's measured 37-mV × 189-ps Eye.** Real transistor Pre-driver switching contributes nontrivial power; the reported supply-power numbers are partial link power and omit original reference/slew control and parasitics. At the other MOS RX bias of 0.95 V, α=0 gave 5/358 vs α=0.3 2/358 for seed A; α>0 often reaches a common error floor of two missing UI, which by itself cannot distinguish good α values.

## 3. Physical MOS reference-offset correction, including both polarities

Physical 180-nm NMOS sink **and PMOS source** devices and a real bias-integrating capacitor were connected to the RX latch threshold-reference node. An **idealized behavioral analog controller** compares measured reference against 250 mV and drives the real MOS current-steering gate voltages. No transmitted data label or receiver error metric enters the correction loop. This is not original Fig. 14 digital calibration FSM or transistor mismatch Monte Carlo.

At α=0.3 and 512-bit PRBS31 / 358 evaluated UI:

| External reference source | Feedback OFF, physical RX errors | Feedback ON, physical RX errors | Final reference V with feedback |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.22 V, negative injected offset | 113/358 | **2/358** | 251.858 mV |
| 0.28 V, positive injected offset | 31/358 | **2/358** | 251.846 mV |
| 0.30 V, larger positive reference | 2/358 | **2/358** | 251.845 mV |

[16-run bidirectional MOS offset experiment](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43255598571). The two residual errors of the corrected cases were missing physical sampling UI rather than incorrect regenerated latches. These results establish physical MOS correction capability over the *tested fixed-bias range*, but not a statistical guarantee across actual foundry mismatch and arbitrary reference offsets.

## 4. Serial-data CDR: serious unresolved physical functional failure

### What is actually connected

- Physical 180-nm MOS TX, surrogate channel, actual regenerative MOS RX latches, actual switched MOS CP and MOS VCO reference PLL.
- Actual XSPICE data-driven sampled **four-XOR digital phase detector**, real XSPICE 128-state 7-bit saturating up/down counter and seven DAC outputs.
- Physical current-starved MOS receiver clock-delay cell driven by an idealized digital-count-to-gate-bias mapping (not a complete transistor-physical phase DAC).
- No transmitted PRBS bit labels are supplied to the data CDR control path; they are only used after the transient for training-frame alignment and heldout scoring.

These are independent reconstructions with no proof of exact P3 Fig. 19 architecture, data/edge sampling alignment, UP/DOWN polarity or digital reset timing.

### Shorter 512-bit pattern and gain

In selected cases the fixed digital counter-to-MOS delay gain +2 mV/code decreased invalid physical MOS sample events (including erasures), such as A -0.05 UI 78/358→49/358 and B -0.05 UI 66/358→34/358. However many erroneous RX samples remained and large gains produced severe sample erasure and counter saturation. [512-bit physical counter scan](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43254164514).

### Longer 1024-bit pattern with **matched common training-only framing**

Four PRBS31 seeds A, B, C, D and two adverse start phases, with gain zero and +3 mV/code, were each simulated through the full 1024-bit MOS transient and **one integer frame lag fitted jointly on training data for both gain settings**. Distinct holdout [256,1014) evaluates 758 UI, counting missing, unresolved and wrong regenerated physical bits. No double-counted same-UI clock was observed.

| PRBS seed | Initial phase (UI) | Control OFF errors / 758 | +3 mV/code CDR ON errors / 758 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | -0.05 | 141 | **184** |
| A | +0.95 | 134 | **179** |
| B | -0.05 | 128 | **194** |
| B | +0.95 | 143 | **178** |
| C | -0.05 | 144 | **171** |
| C | +0.95 | 149 | **194** |
| D | -0.05 | 137 | **219** |
| D | +0.95 | 130 | **164** |

[Original 16 full MOS CDR simulations](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43261667642), [full completeness/matched-control audit](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43262678501), [strict common-frame re-scoring](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43264438487).

**Every one of these eight matched control comparisons worsened** with +3 mV/code for long data packets. Among 16 tested cases, **0/16** produced error-free data recovery. With this gain, the physical MOS receiver clock control counter persistently drifted toward the saturation count 0 and its actuator voltage eventually reached approximately 0.911 V, down from 1.100 V. This supports an UP/DOWN vote bias or phase allocation defect, but not a specific single root cause without further waveform phase alignment.

### Counter modifications (all actually simulated)

- UP+DOWN conflict suppression; no-update on no-transition; both changes: **16 physical MOS 512-bit tests showed no error improvement** under the selected two seeds and two start phases. [Counter-vote-gating ablation](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43263413936).
- Reverse-polarity control gains -0.5/-1/-2/-3 mV/code: **16 actual physical MOS 512-bit simulations, no convincing robust recovery**, most same-as-off or worse. [Negative-gain scan](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43264204864).
- Fine positive gain 0.5/1/1.5/3 mV/code on 512-bit packets: no universal zero-error across the tested two seeds and phases. [Fine gain scan](https://github.com/khi103/EEE6682-Presentation/actions/runs/43256881861).

**Conclusion:** The independent data recovery loop is not fully functional across patterns or acquisition conditions, notwithstanding the **reference PLL's 45/45 PVT qualification**.

- FFE OFF: 339 errors / 212,000 *repeated test-bit observations*.
- FFE α=0.30: 2 errors / 212,000 repeated observations.

## 8. Exact work that remains without obtainable reference data

### Feasible to continue designing but **not successfully validated here**

1. Correct physical serial-data CDR sampling phase relationship and UP/DOWN vote law, implement actual phase DAC / timing adaptation and qualify acquisition and long data patterns with zero erasures and zero wrong regeneration. This requires **new design**, not just more positive-gain searches.
2. Unify MOS receiver full-link PVT: reference PLL is 45/45 at the tested conditions, but actual MOS RX latches fail significantly in FS, SS, SF. Tune predriver, comparator, receiver clock distribution/phase and dynamic offset across actual PDK corners.
3. More robust SPICE transient numeric convergence: default3ps and Gear3ps 45-case PASS for B, but **smaller 1ps/2ps Gear steps failed to finish SS hot low-VDD**. Determine root cause of early ngspice termination (e.g., mixed-mode event convergence, integration tolerances) using complete raw solver logs and alternate simulator.
4. Physical / statistical mismatch and noise: a deterministic synthetic voltage noise test is not foundry device mismatch, independent random jitter, broadband phase noise or a measured BER floor.
5. Full transistor-level Fig. 8 replica impedance calibration, original Fig. 14 comparator and Fig. 18–20 PLL/CDR, actual on-chip Slew Control, Demux and all power supplies. The circuit is an independent reconstruction; only schematic fragments in the paper are available.

## 10. IEEE-style academic-English conclusion (with precise Korean translation)

> An independently reconstructed 3.6-Gb/s CMOS transmitter–receiver chain was evaluated using 0.18-µm transistor-level device models and an XSPICE-controlled reference PLL. Two fixed physical MOS diode-bias PLL implementations each satisfied the predefined frequency, deterministic phase-alignment, control-ripple, and cycle-continuity criteria across 45 process, temperature, and supply-voltage combinations. One design also passed all 45 conditions under an alternative Gear 3-ps integration setting, although numerical convergence at smaller time steps was not established. Transistor-level transmitter pre-emphasis improved the vertical eye opening for two PRBS31 patterns under a shared sampling protocol, with a corresponding increase in partial supply power. Bidirectional physical MOS reference correction also reduced invalid or incorrect receiver decisions under prescribed offset conditions. In contrast, a longer 1024-bit test of the reconstructed four-XOR, 7-bit-counter-driven data-recovery loop failed to establish reliable clock recovery, and the examined positive phase-control gain increased receiver errors in all matched control cases. Consequently, the results substantiate selected circuit principles and quantify important implementation limitations but do not establish circuit-level equivalence, system-level BER, or measured performance of the reported silicon.

**한국어 번역 및 제한적 의미**

> 독립적으로 재구성한 3.6 Gb/s CMOS 송수신 신호 체인을 0.18 µm 트랜지스터 수준 모델과 XSPICE 기반 기준 클록 PLL을 이용해 평가했다. 실제 MOS 다이오드 바이어스 회로가 포함된 두 가지 고정 PLL 설계는 각각 45개 공정, 온도 및 공급전압 조합에서 미리 정의한 주파수, 결정론적 위상 정렬, 제어 전압 리플 및 클록 주기 연속성 기준을 통과했다. 그중 한 설계는 Gear 3 ps 적분 방식에서도 모든 45개 조건을 통과했으나, 더 작은 시간 간격에서의 수치 수렴성은 확인되지 않았다. 실제 트랜지스터 수준 송신 Pre-emphasis는 공통 샘플링 조건에서 두 PRBS31 패턴에 대해 수직 Eye Opening을 개선했으며, 부분 공급 전력의 증가도 동반했다. 양방향 실제 MOS 기준 전압 보정 회로도 설정된 입력 오프셋 조건에서 유효하지 않거나 잘못 판정된 수신 결과를 줄였다. 반면 재구성한 4-XOR 및 7-bit Counter 기반 데이터 복구 루프의 1024비트 장기 시험은 안정적인 클록 복구를 입증하지 못했고, 시험한 양의 위상 제어 이득은 모든 대응 비교에서 오류를 증가시켰다. 따라서 본 결과는 일부 회로 원리를 뒷받침하고 구현상의 중요한 한계를 정량화하지만, 원 논문의 실리콘과의 회로 수준 동일성, 시스템 BER 또는 실측 성능을 입증하지는 않는다.

**표현의 뉘앙스:** "independently reconstructed"는 원 저자의 회로 복제가 아닌 독자적 검증임을 명시한다. "predefined criteria"는 명시된 범위 안에서만 수렴을 주장한다. "deterministic phase-alignment"는 Random RMS Jitter와의 혼동을 방지한다. "partial supply power"는 원 칩의 전체 전력과 구분된다. "failed to establish reliable clock recovery"는 한정된 CDR 모델의 미검증 결과를 정확하게 전달하며, 논문 원저자의 실리콘이 실패했다는 의미가 전혀 아니다.
