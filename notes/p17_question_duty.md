**김형인 — Energy metric**
> The reported efficiency is 2.69 pJ/bit. Which circuit blocks are included in this metric, particularly PLLs, ADC and digital equalization, and how should this analog-efficiency number be compared with the full-link energy of an older electrical transceiver?

Korean nuance: 2.69 pJ/bit가 analog-only라는 자료의 기술에 주의하며, 포함 회로와 수치 비교의 공정성을 묻는다.

**정여진 — Time-interleaved ADC calibration**
> The receiver employs a highly time-interleaved ADC. How are offset, gain, and sampling-time mismatches calibrated, and which residual impairment most strongly limits the measured SNDR and BER?

Korean nuance: 128-way TI-ADC의 회로 mismatch와 보정 잔차가 신호 품질에 끼치는 영향을 구체적으로 묻는다.
